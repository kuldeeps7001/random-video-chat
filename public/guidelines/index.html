Community Guidelines

Help us keep Random Video Chat safe, welcoming, and fun for everyone.

Core Principles

  • Respect others—every screen shows a real person.
  • Keep it clean—avoid explicit, violent, or hateful content.
  • Stay anonymous—do not pressure others for personal info.
  • Report abuse—end the session and disconnect immediately.

Prohibited Behavior

  • Harassment, threats, intimidation.
  • Hate speech, extremist propaganda.
  • Sexual exploitation or nudity.
  • Sharing others' personal data or doxxing.
  • Recording or screenshotting without consent.
  • Automated bots, spam, or flooding.

Safety Tips

  • End sessions that make you uncomfortable.
  • Never share passwords, addresses, or financial data.
  • Use updated browsers to ensure security patches.
  • Be cautious clicking links shared in chat.

Enforcement

We reserve the right to restrict access for repeated or severe violations. Technical measures (rate limits, IP blocks) may be applied when necessary.

Improving Moderation

Future updates may introduce reporting tools, automated detection, and filter controls for safer experiences.

Contact

To raise concerns or suggest improvements: support@example.com

These guidelines are a baseline and should be adapted as features evolve.
