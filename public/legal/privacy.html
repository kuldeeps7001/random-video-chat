Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 17, 2025

1. Overview

This platform enables anonymous one-to-one video conversations using peer‑to‑peer WebRTC technology. We intentionally minimize data collection and do not require user accounts.

2. Data We (Do Not) Collect

  • No account data: We do not store usernames, emails, or passwords.
  • No chat/video recordings: Media streams go directly between users (P2P) and are not recorded.
  • Temporary socket IDs are used only for establishing sessions.
  • Minimal logs: Server logs may contain connection timestamps and non-identifying technical events for abuse detection.

3. Sessions & Media

Video and audio are exchanged via WebRTC. When P2P cannot be established (e.g., strict NAT), a TURN relay may forward encrypted media packets but does not store them.

4. Cookies & Tracking

We avoid persistent cookies except those strictly necessary for load balancing or security. We do not use third‑party advertising trackers.

5. Abuse & Safety

We may temporarily retain IP addresses or connection metadata if needed to investigate severe abuse, fraud, or security threats.

6. Third‑Party Services

STUN/TURN infrastructure may be provided by reputable third parties (e.g., Google STUN, commercial TURN providers). Their transient processing of connection metadata is governed by their own policies.

7. Children

This platform is not directed to children under 13. If you believe a child is using the service improperly, contact us for removal and further action.

8. Your Choices

  • Decline camera/microphone permissions—no video session will start.
  • Terminate the session at any time using the Stop button.
  • Clear your browser data to remove any local cached assets.

9. Changes

We may update this Policy periodically. Continued use after changes indicates acceptance.

10. Contact

For privacy inquiries, email: privacy@example.com
